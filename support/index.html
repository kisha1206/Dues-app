Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting bill reminders
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Dues in your device settings.
  • In the app, check the reminder time on the bill and that reminders are on in Settings.
  • Android: some phones stop background apps to save battery. Go to Settings > Apps > Dues > Battery and choose Unrestricted.
  • iPhone: make sure a Focus mode isn't silencing Dues.
Can I use Dues on both Android and iPhone?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on each device and your bills, income and shared bills sync between them.

My bills aren't showing on a new device

Make sure you signed in with the same method you used before, such as the same Google account, Apple ID or email address. Signing in a different way creates a separate account.

What does Premium include, and how much is it?

The free version lets you track a limited number of bills. Premium removes the limit and unlocks the extra features listed on the upgrade screen in the app, where you'll also see the current price in your currency.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?
  • Android: open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions and choose Dues.
  • iPhone: open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions and choose Dues.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Premium but the app still shows the free version

Open the Premium (Upgrade) screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, while signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought with. If that doesn't help, email us with your order ID from the purchase receipt.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Google and Apple, so refunds are requested from them: through Google Play's refund page or Apple's Report a Problem page.

How does splitting a bill work?

Open a bill, choose to split it, and add the people you're sharing it with. You can mark who has paid, and everyone involved can see the shared bill.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings > Delete Account. You can also request deletion without the app.
