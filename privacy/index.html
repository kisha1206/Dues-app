Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Applies to Dues: Bill Tracker & Reminder on Android and iOS

Dues ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Pawan Kumar. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what the app needs to store and sync your bills, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

1. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and, if you add them, your phone number, bio and profile photo. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address.
  • Bills and finances: the bills you add (name, amount, category, due date, repeat schedule, paid status, notes), your payment history, and the income entries you record. Dues does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank or card details.
  • People you split bills with: the names, and any phone numbers or email addresses, of people you add to a split bill, and who has paid.
  • Family Plan: if you create or join a household, its members and the bills shared within it.
  • Preferences: your currency, reminder times, notification settings and other in-app settings.
  • Support messages: anything you send us by email.

Data collected automatically

  • Usage statistics: which screens and features are used, collected with Firebase Analytics. This is not linked to your name or email and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Crash reports: device model, operating system version, app version and technical details of the crash, collected with Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Notification token: a device identifier from Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver reminders and household updates.
  • Subscription status: if you buy Premium, Google Play or the App Store tells us which plan you have and whether it's active. We never see your payment card details.

Voice input

When you add a bill by voice, your speech is converted to text on your device. On Android, the bill details are also extracted from that text on your device. On iPhone, the text transcript (not the audio recording) may be sent to our server, which uses an AI service to extract the bill name, amount and date and returns them to the app. Audio recordings never leave your device, and the transcript is used only to fill in the bill.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the app: store your bills, sync them across your devices, and share split bills and household bills with the people you choose.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you've turned on.
  • To manage your Premium subscription.
  • To fix crashes and understand which features are used, so we can improve the app.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run the app on our behalf, and with the people you choose to share bills with.

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be located outside your country. Google protects it under its own security and data-processing terms.

4. Device permissions

Dues asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can turn any of them off in your device settings.

  • Notifications: to remind you before bills are due.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: for adding bills by voice.
  • Contacts (iPhone): to pick people to split a bill with. Only the contacts you choose are saved; your address book is not uploaded.
  • Photos: to set a profile photo, you pick one with your device's photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose, not your photo library.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account data, bills and income are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your account and app data. Anything left over is removed from our systems within 30 days.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are kept for the standard retention period of Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play or Apple as required for billing and tax.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile, bills and income in the app at any time.
  • Deletion: delete individual items in the app, or delete your whole account under Settings > Delete Account. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
  • A copy of your data: email us and we'll send it to you.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or India), you may have further rights: to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us from the address on your account. We'll respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest on Google Cloud. Access rules on our database ensure that only you, and the people you've shared a bill or household with, can read your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details and device safe.

8. Children

Dues is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
