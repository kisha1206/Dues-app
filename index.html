What Dues does

Your bills, handled.

Add a bill once and Dues keeps track of it: when it's due, whether it's paid, and how much is left after it.

Recurring bills

Set a bill to repeat weekly, fortnightly, monthly or yearly, and it comes back on its own.

Reminders before due dates

Choose how early to be reminded, from the same day to a month ahead, at a time that suits you.

Income and monthly net

Log income next to your bills and see what's left for the month, with insights on where it goes.

Split bills

Share a bill with family or friends and see who has paid, without chasing anyone.

Add by voice

Say "Internet, sixty dollars, due on the fifth" and Dues fills in the bill for you.

Home screen widgets

See what's due next without opening the app, on Android and iPhone.
