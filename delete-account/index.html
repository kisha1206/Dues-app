Delete your Dues account

Dues: Bill Tracker & Reminder, by Pawan Kumar

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Dues and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

For your security, deletion needs a recent sign-in. If the app asks, sign out, sign back in, and try again.

Request deletion without the app

If you can't use the app, email er.pwndhull07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Dues account", sent from the email address you use with Dues. We'll confirm the request and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email address, phone number, bio and profile photo
  • All your bills, bill splits, recurring bills and payment history
  • Your income entries
  • People you added to split bills with
  • A Family Plan household you own (if you joined someone else's household, you're removed from it)
  • Your preferences and notification settings

What is kept

  • Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store, as required for billing and tax. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are not linked to your name or email, and are deleted automatically by our analytics provider within its standard retention period.

Delete some of your data without deleting your account

You can delete individual items at any time in the app:

  • Bills: swipe a bill on the Home screen, or open it and delete it.
  • Income: swipe an entry in the Finance tab to delete it.
  • Profile photo and details: Settings > Edit Profile.

To have other specific data deleted, email er.pwndhull07@gmail.com and tell us what you'd like removed.
